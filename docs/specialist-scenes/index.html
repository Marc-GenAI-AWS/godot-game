WHOLE SCENES FROM ONE LINE

Each scene below started as one sentence. Claude Fable 5.1 (the director) planned the sky, the ground and the vegetation together; three fine-tuned 3B models (Qwen2.5-Coder-3B, one per segment) wrote those layers on a local GPU; the verifier rendered each layer in the game and a Claude judge scored it, sending failures back to the same 3B model for up to three rounds. Segments that were not accepted keep the normal game layer. No layer was written or edited by a larger model. Click a scene to play it with its accepted layers.

What this does not show yet. Each layer is judged on its own, inside an otherwise normal scene; nothing yet checks the assembled scene as a whole. That is why the overcast scene keeps the bright normal sky under its overcast brief: a composite verifier that judges the whole scene and sends problems back to the right specialist is being built next. Across these three scenes 6 of 9 layers were accepted (vegetation 3 of 3, sky 1 of 3, ground 1 of 3). Single-segment sky examples are on the specialist skies page.
