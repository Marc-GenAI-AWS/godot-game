Skies written by a fine-tuned 3B model

Each sky below came from the one line of description on its card. Claude turns the line into a sky brief, a fine-tuned Qwen2.5-Coder-3B writes the Godot 4 sky layer, and the verifier renders it in the running game and judges it against the brief. No sky here was written or edited by a larger model.

Only the sky is generated here. Every other layer is the hand-built scene, so you are seeing one model’s work in isolation. The whole-scene demos, where the ground, planting and furniture are model-written too, are on the scenes page.
