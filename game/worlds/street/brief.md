# Street Drive — world brief

References: `examples/man-driving-car-down-street/`, `examples/car-driving/`,
`examples/car-driving-2/` (driving family), plus the street-walk clips for
the on-foot variant later.

## Global

- Axes: road runs along Z; the player drives toward -Z in the right-hand lane
  (x = -1.9). Houses and verges either side; downtown far to -X.
- Camera: chase, ~7 m behind, ~2.4 m up, wide lens, slight look-ahead.
- Light: hard sun, blue sky with cumulus, long shadows from palms and lamps.

## Per segment

| Segment | Brief |
|---|---|
| ground | Grey asphalt, double yellow centre line, white edge lines, crosswalk and stop line per block, kerbs, concrete sidewalks with joints, grass verges. |
| architecture | Garden walls with pillars and gates, one/two-storey houses with pitched roofs and chimneys set back on lots, downtown towers on the far horizon. |
| vegetation | Tall thin fan palms and round leafy trees in the verge, hedges along lot fronts. |
| props | Street lamps, stop sign at the crosswalk, bins, hydrants, a mailbox, a bus bench. |
| vehicles | Procedural low-poly cars (hatch, sedan, suv, pickup); parked along both kerbs; traffic in both lanes. Player car: small yellow hatchback with a black roof. |
| crowd | Pedestrians on sidewalks in casual clothes, walking and standing. |
| player | Vehicle mode: accelerate / brake / reverse / steer; stays on the road; soft collisions with cars. |
