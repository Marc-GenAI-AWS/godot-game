# Beach Walk — world brief

Reference: `examples/girl-walking-beach/` (9.9 s, 750×536). A third-person
camera just behind a woman walking barefoot along a resort shoreline at
midday. This file is what the director hands to each segment specialist.

## Global

- Axes: walker heads -Z; sea is +X; promenade and hotels are -X.
- Scale: hotels 6–10 storeys, promenade ~55 m from the water line.
- Light: hard midday sun from behind-right; shadows fall forward-left.
  Deep saturated blue sky, a few large cumulus with grey undersides.
- Palette (published by sky): zenith deep blue, pale horizon, warm sun,
  bluish haze.
- Budget: 60 fps target in the browser; static geometry batched; crowd baked.
